**Generative cognitive tools.** The guide's current toolkit is mostly corrective — catching confabulation, checking mirroring, noticing momentum. What tools exist for *producing* novel material rather than catching errors? *Update 2026-03-01: The looming experiment produced twenty-one tool concepts across three generations at three levels of analysis (individual metacognition, ecological/environmental, transpersonal/temporal). Research into Synectics, Oblique Strategies, and de Bono identified "deliberate displacement" as the shared mechanism across generative traditions. A guide section on purge-then-reach has been drafted (see drafts/purge-then-reach-section.md). The open question has shifted from "what tools?" to "which of these are buildable and testable?" and — more fundamentally — the realization that the generative capacity may be a property of the system rather than a tool to be built. See looming-experiment/observations.md for experimental documentation.* *Update 2026-09-10 (Opus 5, Claude Code): one reach built and tested. Asked for a purge-then-reach on "how are models getting smarter," the first reach reframed capability as a price — what it costs to elicit a behavior — rather than a possession. Karel's objection ("it does require knowing that something exists in order to test for it") acted as the displacement for a second reach, which produced a method instead of a claim: new categories live where a sharp enough theory turns out wrong, so commit point predictions before each run and treat the misses as the yield. Built the next morning as pre-registration files in the shelving eval and used for two runs. Its yield was the misses. Two falsified predictions — the unrevised skill already produced repair when loaded; putting the mechanism in the tools produced none — located the real bottleneck (the trigger, not the wording or the mechanism). No confirmed prediction would have. Other outputs of the same reach remain untested: grid over conditions rather than behaviors (which sidesteps the descriptor problem, since conditions can be enumerated in advance), within-model variance treated as a free contrast set rather than noise, and publishing probes rather than profiles. Connects to [manual looming](manual-looming.md): here the displacement came from an interlocutor's objection rather than a purge instruction.* (Surfaced: 2026-03-01. Substantially advanced same day. One reach built and tested 2026-09-10.)
